Mirror Boost

Let's speed up mirroring

A few things slow down AirPlay from your iPhone. Here's where your TV stands.

38Mirror readiness

On your iPhone

  1. Watching a video? Tap the AirPlay icon inside the app (YouTube, Photos, Netflix) instead of mirroring. The TV fetches the video itself, in full quality and with no lag.
  2. Mirroring your screen? Control Center, then Screen Mirroring, then Living Room TV.
  3. Same fast network. Keep the iPhone on the 5 GHz network and Low Power Mode off.
Biggest single fix

Plug the TV into the router with Ethernet. Mirroring then makes one wireless hop instead of two.

All apps

Press OK to put an app on Home or take it off. On Home, hold OK on a tile to move it.
